# Bike Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes bike sales data to explore customer purchasing patterns, sales trends, and demographic insights. An interactive dashboard was developed to support data-driven business decisions and improve understanding of sales performance.

## Problem Statement
Understanding customer purchasing behavior is essential for improving sales performance and identifying business growth opportunities. However, raw sales data can make it difficult to identify which customer groups are more likely to purchase bicycles and how factors such as age, income, commute distance, and demographic characteristics relate to buying decisions.

## Methodology
1. **Data cleaning:** Reviewed the dataset, checked for missing values and inconsistencies, and prepared the data for analysis.
2. **Data transformation:** Organized customer attributes and created suitable categories for comparing purchasing behavior.
3. **Exploratory data analysis:** Examined relationships between customer demographics, lifestyle characteristics, and bike purchase decisions.
4. **Dashboard development:** Created interactive visualizations and summary metrics to present customer purchasing patterns.
5. **Insight generation:** Interpreted the results to identify potential customer segments and opportunities for targeted marketing.
